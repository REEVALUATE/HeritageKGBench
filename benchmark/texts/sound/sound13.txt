"Bon Daw Bwe Pat Pyo 'Konbaung Parame"" / 'Fame of the Konbaung Dynasty' is the title of this composition. In this praise song, the last Burmese monarchy (1752-1885) is glorified, which is sometimes also named after its first king, Alaungpaya (1752-1760). The British colonial powers
sent the last monarch, King Thibaw (1878-1885), into exile in India in 1885. The great poet, mu-sician, intellectual, statesman, and military expert, Myawaddy Mingyi U Sa (1766-1839), whose long life was at once rich and also riddled with terrible blows of fate and which spanned the reign of eight of the eleven kings of the dynasty, composed this piece to be performed at the king's court in Amarapura. Along with the most complex and virtuosic pieces in the classical repertoire, it belongs to the genre of pat pyo. Its performance requires a grasp of a large part of the old court songs. The pat pyo experienced its heyday toward the end of the 18th century. Besides the princes Pyin Si and U Kyin U, Myawaddy Mingyi U Sa is considered its most important representative.
