Cup awarded as a prize in the Marathon Race of the “Athens 1906” Intercalated Games. Front view inscription “INTERNATIONAL OLYMPIC GAMES IN ATHENS 1906”. Back view inscription “ENTHYMEMA MARATHONIOU” (SOUVENIR OF THE MARATHON RUN)&#xA;The Cup - Marathon Race Commemorative was a key document of the Exhibition: "Olympism. A Modern Invention, an Ancient Heritage" (24.04 - 16.09.2024) implemented by the Louvre Museum in collaboration with the French School of Athens in the framework of the Olympic and Paralympic Games "Paris 2024".&#xA;
