Reproduction of a medal from the Zappian Games of 1870. Front view inscription "ΓΕΩΡΓΙΟΣ Α! ΒΑΣΙΛΕΥΣ ΤΩΝ ΕΛΛΗΝΩΝ" - "GEORGE A! KING OF THE GREEKS" and in the center has a representation of him. On the reverse side is depicted an olive wreath and is inscribed on the inside "ΑΓΩΝΟΘΕΤΗΣ ΕΥΑΓΓΕΛΗΣ ΖΑΓΓΑΣ, ΟΛΥΜΠΙΑ ΑΘΗΝΗΣΙ ΑΩΟ΄ "(ΖΑΠΠΕΙΑ ΟΛΥΜΠΙΑ) - "GAMES SETTER EVAGELIS ZAGAS, OLYMPIA ATHENS AΩO" (ZAPPEIA OLYMPIA)
