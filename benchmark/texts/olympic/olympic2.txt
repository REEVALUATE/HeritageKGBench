Letter from Baron Pierre de Coubertin 14-6-1894 to I. Fokianos expressing his great joy and gratitude as an honorary member of the P.G.S. as well as his joy for the presence at the conference of the distinguished man D. Vikelas at the suggestion of the P.G.S. The above typed caption is available in Greek, English and French, below the handwritten letter. The letterhead has the inscription "CONGRES INTERNATIONAL ATHLETIQUE DE PARIS", "16-24 JUIN 1894" printed on the obverse.
