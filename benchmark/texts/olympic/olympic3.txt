A four-page letter dated 18-6-1894 from D. Vikelas to Ioannis Fokianos, informing him of the work of the Congress, announcing his election as President, following a unanimous decision of the plenary session of the Congress, of the committee in charge of the revival of the Olympic Games and informing him of his intention to propose ATHENS for the holding of the First International Olympic Games. The above typed caption is available in Greek, English and French, below the handwritten letter.
